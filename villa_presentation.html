The brief, and what it got

The brief was 4,500 sq ft of house on 7,500 sq ft of land, with an overflow pool, a garden, a garage forecourt, a gate and a stone boundary wall. Four bedrooms. A women's majlis, and no men's dewaniya. Family living on the ground floor.

The set below delivers 4654 sq ft on 7,494 — 3 % over on the house, on the nose for the land — at 32.5 % site coverage, inside the 25–35 % a plot this size is normally held to. Both figures are measured off the wall polygons the drawings are made from, not typed in.

How the set was proved

The first version of this villa was critiqued by a fleet of agents across six lenses. It cost roughly 7.4 million tokens and returned 160 findings — 52 of them fatal. Reading them back, almost every confirmed finding was mechanically detectable: areas are arithmetic, an inner room is a graph traversal, bathrooms-per-bedroom is counting.

So the design was rewritten as data, and the fifteen rules below were written against it. They run in about a second and cost nothing. Agents are worth their price on judgement that cannot be computed — whether an elevation reads as architect-designed — and nothing else.

Rules 13 to 15 were added while loading the set into AutoCAD, and each one found something the previous fourteen had passed. That is the honest argument for writing checks: not that the current set is complete, but that every new one is permanent.

  1. 01
    Opening penetration

    Every door and window void must be deeper than the wall it cuts. A void sized to the wall face leaves a skin and cuts nothing.

    This is the class that produced 52 fatal findings in the first scheme.

  2. 02
    Door lands where it says

    A door's threshold strip must fall in the room it names, not inside a sub-room nested there.

    Found three doors opening into an en-suite or dressing room instead of the bedroom.

  3. 03
    Reachability

    A graph over every door from the entry. Any room not reached is fatal.

    Zero on the first run, because the data makes it hard to get wrong.

  4. 04
    No inner rooms

    Re-run the graph with all other bedrooms deleted. A bedroom you can only reach through another bedroom fails.

    Drove the single-corridor replan of the first floor.

  5. 05
    A bathroom per bedroom

    Four bedrooms, four en-suites, each doored from its own bedroom.

  6. 06
    Areas against researched minima

    Measured on the NET polygon — the room less anything nested in it and less the partition between them.

    The master bedroom was reading 27.7 m² while actually being 15.5. A comment had also swallowed two entries in the minima table, so bedrooms 2 and 3 had no area check at all.

  7. 07
    Overlap and voids

    Rooms may not partially overlap; full containment is read as a nested sub-room. No void may cut a habitable room.

    Caught the stair well landing inside the relocated master bedroom.

  8. 08
    Pool

    Crest 2–6 mm below top water, basin cut below the crest, terrace ≥ 1200 mm.

    4 mm differential, 1200 mm terrace.

  9. 09
    Forecourt

    A car must stand clear of the street between gate line and house.

    5200 mm — a car fits; the comfort figure is 5500–6000. The one note the set still carries.

  10. 10
    Stair

    Flights must fit the well; rise ≤ 220 mm; 2R + G between 550 and 700.

    20 risers at 205 mm, 2R+G = 660. A dog-leg, because 19 goings at 250 will not fit a 4050 landing.

  11. 11
    Circulation share

    Dedicated corridor ≤ 8 % of gross internal area.

  12. 12
    Furniture

    Every one of the 49 blocks inside its room, clear of every other block, clear of every door swing, with 550 mm at a WC or basin and 700 mm to one side of a bath or bed.

    Found two en-suites too small to hold a bath at all. Both were enlarged.

  13. 13
    An opening must sit ON a wall

    Intersect every void with the derived wall fabric. Check 01 proves the void is deep enough; this proves it is in the right place.

    Twenty-five openings were cutting thin air — every external window and two doors, left behind by an 800 mm plan shift. They were the right thickness, so nothing else had noticed.

  14. 14
    Rooms must share a wall

    An internal door's two rooms must be exactly one partition apart.

    A door was declared between the show kitchen and the formal dining room. They are two rooms apart — the pantry sits between them.

  15. 15
    Walls exist where rooms are nested

    A nested room is carved out of its host together with a partition band, so there is real wall for its door to sit in.

    Without it there was no wall at all between a bedroom and its en-suite, and a 550–1050 mm strip between the north block and the south wing belonged to no room, which the fabric read as a metre of solid masonry.

The one note the set still carries. The forecourt is 5,200 mm between gate line and house. A car fits; the researched comfort figure is 5,500–6,000. Buying it back means shortening the pool below 6.0 m, at which point the water stops reading as a pool. It is a trade, made deliberately, and it is on the drawing rather than buried.

What is actually in AutoCAD

The drawings are authored by ezdxf and the model is built from the same room polygons; AutoCAD is used only for what it alone provides — inserting real content blocks and holding the DWG. Nothing is drawn twice.

Both sheets were then measured back out of AutoCAD. Every block's bounding box is compared against the footprint the plan demands: blocks are scaled uniformly, never distorted to fit, so placement must be exact and size is allowed the residual that leaves.

The model was probed rather than admired. A 500 mm sphere is dropped at the centre of every room and intersected with the solid: a room must return nothing, a wall must return the whole sphere. The first version of that probe swallowed an exception and scored every point as void — it reported 19/19 on a model it had never read. A check that cannot fail is worse than no check.

Materials

Four materials, one 300 mm setting-out grid. Stone joint, ceiling joint, door head, window head and skirting line all land on it. That discipline, not the cost of any single item, is what reads as expensive.

The move worth protecting above everything else on the page: the same travertine runs from the family living room straight out onto the pool terrace at one level, with a 20 mm slot drain at the threshold and no upstand, and the glazing sits in that slot.

The pool, in numbers that decide it

An overflow edge is a tolerance problem, not a plumbing one. The crest sits 4 mm below top water level; that differential, not the pump, sets the thickness of the sheet. A 25 mm error destroys the effect.

The number that decides whether the pool works at all is the level tolerance: ±0.8 mm over the whole perimeter, set with a rotating laser during the pour and again at tiling.

Balance volume was built up honestly rather than taken from a rule of thumb — bather displacement, water in transit, backwash and surge come to 2,510 L against a 1,612 L rule-of-thumb cross-check, so the tank is sized on the larger.
